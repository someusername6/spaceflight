# Campaign & Progression Review

## Campaign Structure

### Sectors
- 5 sectors of increasing difficulty
- Sector 5 is "Endless" mode (repeatable)
- Advancement cost: 1000 × sector number

### Deployment Limits
- Increases per sector (4-6 ships max)
- Encourages squad growth over time

### Permadeath (Ironman Mode)
- Optional setting locked at campaign creation
- Campaign ends when commander dies
- Non-ironman allows mission restart

## Mission Types

### Elimination (Default)
- Destroy all enemies across waves
- Fixed 100% reward
- Wave-based spawning with delays

### Escort
- Protect convoy ships reaching escape point
- Reward scales with convoy survival percentage
- Enemies spawn continuously perpendicular to path
- Wingmen set to defensive mode

### Station Defense
- Protect station from destruction
- Reward scales with station hull remaining
- Wave + reinforcement triggers
- Different station types (mining/refinery/military)

### Ambush
- Attack enemy convoy
- 100% reward for stopped, 50% for destroyed
- Convoy stops when no escorts nearby
- Time pressure from escape timer

### Attack Station
- Destroy enemy station
- Flat reward per difficulty
- Initial defenders + reinforcement waves
- Overwhelming wave at 180s (soft time limit)

## Economy

### Credits
- Earned from mission rewards
- Spent on: ships, weapons, ammo, pilots, sector advance

### Salvage
- 0-10 scrap per destroyed ship
- Chance for weapon drops
- Percentage of remaining ammo recovered

### Store
- Finite inventory per sector
- Trickle restock after missions
- Items unlock by sector

## Pilot System

### Initial Squad
- Commander (ace skill)
- 3 wingmen (regular skill)

### Recruits
- 4 initial, 3-5 refresh per mission
- Skill distribution scales with sector
- Pricing: rookie=75cr to elite=1200cr

### Pilot Stats
- Kills, assists, missions flown/won
- Damage dealt/received

## Strengths

1. **Clear Progression** - Sectors provide milestone goals
2. **Diverse Missions** - 5 types with distinct mechanics
3. **Meaningful Choices** - Squad composition matters
4. **Risk/Reward** - Ironman mode adds tension
5. **Deterministic** - Fair replay support

## Areas for Improvement

1. **Limited Mid-Game Goals** - Sectors feel similar after first few missions
2. **Pilot Attachment** - No personality/story for pilots
3. **Economy Simplicity** - Credits are the only currency
4. **No Side Objectives** - Missions are straightforward
5. **Replay Mode Penalty** - 50% reward makes grinding tedious

## Recommendations

1. Add sector-specific events or story beats
2. Give pilots backstories or random traits
3. Consider secondary resources (reputation, intel)
4. Add optional objectives within missions
5. Reduce replay penalty to 75% or add variety bonus
